### Why this is the control, and why it was not re-executed by hand

A negative control asserts that the test detects the defect — that it fails when the repair is absent and passes when present. That is exactly the pairing above: same test, same focused filter, same suite size, across the single commit that introduced the repair, with conductor-issued receipts on both sides.

Re-running by mutating and restoring the source would produce the same logical content with weaker evidence: a hand-run rather than a conductor receipt. It would also require dirtying a registered goal worktree, which blocks dispatch for **every** goal on the board while the build and test run — ten lanes were live at the time. The recorded pairing is both stronger and cheaper.
