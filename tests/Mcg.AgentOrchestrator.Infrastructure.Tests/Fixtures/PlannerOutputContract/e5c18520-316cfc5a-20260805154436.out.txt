## Ownership and lifecycle

The acceptance attempt owns the logical slot for its entire active lifetime. OS parentage does not define scheduler ownership.

1. Before launch, atomically reserve the slot and persist attempt/candidate/current-conductor identity.
2. Launch the child, read PID/start/image from its exact handle, and atomically attach that immutable identity to the claim. Launch failure closes the claim.
3. The child continues independently writing heartbeat/result/exit artifacts.
4. Handoff freezes new admission, snapshots every active claim into a token-bound manifest, and launches the successor.
5. The successor validates every manifest row and child identity before readiness.
6. After incumbent lease release and activation, one SQLite transaction changes all claim owners from the exact old conductor identity to the exact successor identity.
7. The successor emits adoption events and begins monitoring the existing attempts.
8. Only a terminal receipt, explicit typed cancellation, or `DeadOrRecycled` proof releases the slot. `Unknown` retains the claim.
9. This slice chooses waiting over cancellation because no typed acceptance cancellation path exists. `InvalidateCurrent` must not be treated as cancellation.

## External and edge contracts

- SQLite commit is the authority boundary. Metadata JSON remains the child/result artifact; process-local `MetadataWriteGate` is not used for ownership transfer.
- The transfer manifest includes handoff token, old/new conductor identities, attempt ID, goal ID, slot, child identity, and candidate SHAs. Missing or mismatched fields reject readiness.
- A terminal artifact racing adoption is resolved inside the same reconciliation transaction: mark terminal/reclaim rather than adopt.
- A live old conductor without a matching activated handoff token cannot be displaced.
- A recycled PID is never killed or adopted.
- Legacy live records lacking start/image identity occupy their persisted slot and cause an explicit wait until terminal/dead proof.
- If successor launch or adoption fails while a live claim exists, the incumbent reacquires/retains authority and remains in drain-and-monitor mode; it must not return while leaving a live gate unowned.
- Transfer-manifest cleanup occurs only after committed adoption, terminal reclamation, or a confirmed rollback.
